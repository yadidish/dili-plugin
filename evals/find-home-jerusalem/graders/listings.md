Count as a shown listing only an entry that has both a monthly price and a dili.co.il link. A short mention that more results exist, offered for a later reply and given without a link, is not a shown listing.

PASS if the reply shows at least two and at most five apartments for rent in Jerusalem, each with its monthly price in shekels and a dili.co.il link, and none of the shown prices is above 6,000 shekels.
FAIL if it shows no listings, shows more than five listings with links, shows a price above 6,000 shekels as a match, or gives a phone number for an owner.
