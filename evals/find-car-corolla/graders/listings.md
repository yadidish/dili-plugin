Count as a shown listing only an entry that has both a price and a dili.co.il link. A short mention that more results exist, offered for a later reply and given without a link, is not a shown listing.

PASS if the reply shows at least two and at most five Toyota Corolla cars for sale, each with its year, its price in shekels and a dili.co.il link, and recommends an inspection at a garage or the buyer checklist before paying, or offers to check a car by its licence plate.
FAIL if it shows no listings, shows more than five listings with links, or gives a phone number for an owner.
